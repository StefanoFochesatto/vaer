Pillar 01 • Physical Observation

Advanced Sensing Technologies

Measuring environmental variables reliably in extreme, remote, and complex field conditions. High-fidelity observations form the empirical anchor of every physical model.

Extreme-Environment Instrumentation

Conventional commercial sensors fail when subjected to sustained -40°C temperatures, rime icing, sub-zero battery degradation, and lightning-induced surges on exposed mountain crests.

  • Sub-zero Power Management: High-efficiency lithium-titanate and hybrid supercapacitor power stages optimized for continuous low-irradiance arctic winter operation.
  • Distributed Snowpack Thermal Probes: High-precision thermistor strings with calibrated 0.05°C differential sensitivity to measure vertical temperature gradients across snow stratigraphy.
  • Low-Power Mesh Communications: Redundant sub-GHz LoRa mesh and burst Iridium satellite transceivers capable of piercing high-relief mountain topography.

Airborne Atmospheric Soundings

Bridging the gap between surface weather stations and coarse orbital satellite measurements via vertical thermodynamic and aerosol profiling.

  • Thermodynamic Sensor Suite: Millisecond-response platinum resistance thermometers (PT100) and capacitive hygrometers georeferenced to centimeter-level RTK GNSS.
  • Isokinetic Sampling Inlets: Custom aerodynamically contoured intake nozzles designed to sample ambient air without particle fractionation or rotor-induced vortex distortion.
  • Optical Particulate Counters (OPC): Laser-scattering spectrometers calibrated for continuous sizing across PM1, PM2.5, and PM10 aerodynamic diameter bins.

Pillar 02 • Digital Physical Systems

Computational Physics & Numerical Methods

Translating environmental observations and physical understanding into quantitative, continuous representations of complex Earth-system processes.

Navier-Stokes Surface Winds & Mesoscale Flow

Mountain topography drastically accelerates, channels, and separates atmospheric flow. Standard weather forecasts operate at 3km–12km resolution, failing to capture ridge-line loading and lee-slope vortex shedding.

  • Terrain-Conforming Coordinate Systems: Numerical discretization of the Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS) equations over high-resolution digital elevation models (DEMs).
  • Subgrid-Scale Turbulence Closures: Accounting for surface roughness, thermal stratification, and katabatic mountain breezes.
  • Snow Mass Transport: Modeling saltation, suspension, and deposition fluxes across avalanche trigger zones.

Real-Time Data Assimilation (4D-Var & EnKF)

Observations alone are sparse point samples. Physical models alone drift from reality. Data assimilation mathematically fuses both, weighted by their respective covariance matrices.

  • Cost Function Minimization: J(x) = (x - x_b)^T B^-1 (x - x_b) + (y - H(x))^T R^-1 (y - H(x))
  • Background Covariance Modeling (B): Propagating spatial correlation lengths across mountain valleys and inversion boundaries.
  • Nonlinear Observation Operators (H): Mapping sparse thermistor and drone readings into the complete 3D atmospheric thermodynamic state vector.

Thermodynamic Stratification & Inversion Physics

During Arctic and subarctic winter, longwave radiative cooling creates surface-based thermal inversions (dT/dz > 0) that decouple the pit floor from synoptic winds, trapping deadly particulates.

  • Atmospheric Hydrostatic & Geostrophic Equilibrium: Solvers for pressure gradient balance in deep open-pit geometry.
  • Radiative Cooling & Heat Flux Budgets: Modeling downwelling longwave radiation, sensible heat exchange, and ground conduction.
  • Subsidence Dynamics: Anticyclonic high-pressure downward air motion acting as a rigid lid over stagnant valley air.

Snowpack Thermal Diffusion & Metamorphism

Temperature gradients within snow drive water vapor sublimation and recrystallization, creating brittle kinetic facets and depth hoar—the foundation of catastrophic slab avalanches.

  • 1D/3D Thermal Diffusion PDE: ρ c_p (∂T/∂t) = ∂/∂z [ k_eff (∂T/∂z) ] + Q_latent
  • Kinetic Growth Criteria: Automated detection of vapor pressure gradients exceeding the critical threshold (> 10°C/m).
  • Settlement & Creep Rheology: Viscoelastic constitutive laws tracking progressive shear stress accumulation under overlying slabs.
Pillar 03 • Operational Guidance

Predictive Systems & Decision Support

Turning observations and numerical results into calibrated risk probabilities, early warnings, and interpretable outputs for operational stakeholders.

Operational Dashboards

Designed for shift supervisors and forecasters. High-contrast interfaces displaying live sensor health, model convergence status, and clear risk flags without clutter.

Uncertainty Envelopes

No single deterministic number. We deliver confidence bounds and probability distributions, communicating explicitly when observations are sparse or atmospheric stability is ambiguous.

Frontline Action Feeds

Automated threshold alerts via SMS, sat-radio relays, and enterprise SCADA webhooks to inform shift dispatch, blast scheduling, and road closure protocols.

How the Pillars Work Together: The Full System Architecture

The three pillars form an unbroken loop. Sensing collects ground truth; Computational Physics assimilates data into dynamical conservation equations; Predictive Systems extracts risk thresholds; Operational Decisions close the loop by triggering field validation and targeted sensor re-tasking.
