The technology framework

Physical observations.
Useful predictions.

Advanced Sensing Technologies, Computational Physics, and Predictive Systems are fully interrelated: observations inform models, and models inform operational guidance.

01 / Integration

The complete chain.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Collect environmental measurements using field instrumentation and drone-based sensing.

  2. 02

    Reconstruct

    Combine observations through real-time processing, integration, and data assimilation.

  3. 03

    Model

    Represent governing processes with physics-based and data-driven models.

  4. 04

    Predict

    Produce forecasts, risk information, and estimates of inversion likelihood and duration.

  5. 05

    Decide

    Provide interpretable guidance for operational planning, with human judgment and uncertainty quantification.

02 / Advanced Sensing Technologies

Observe the physical system.

Custom environmental instrumentation and sensor development for demanding field conditions.

Instrumentation
Weather and snowpack temperature sensing, including distributed measurements in mountainous terrain.
Electronics & communications
Embedded electronics, low-power computing, and rugged communications systems.
Atmospheric observations
Drone profiling of pressure, temperature, and relative humidity; aerosol instrumentation for PM1, PM2.5, and PM10.
Remote sensing
Active and passive laser/microwave sensing and field-deployed systems designed for high-resolution observations.

03 / Computational Physics

Reconstruct and model.

Quantitative representations of environmental processes, built around physical constraints and domain knowledge.

Numerical methods
Advanced numerical methods, computational mathematics, and scientific software workflows.
Physical models
Physics-based and hybrid physical/statistical models; coupled atmosphere, snow, ice, and land-surface processes.
Wind & mesoscale modeling
High-resolution surface-wind fields and mesoscale modeling.
Data assimilation
Real-time data processing, integration, and assimilation to connect observations with models.

04 / Predictive Systems

Make the output useful.

Translate observations and computational results into forecasts, risk information, and operational decision support.

Monitoring & prediction
Integrated environmental monitoring and prediction platforms.
Decision support
Avalanche-related tools and forecasts of inversion occurrence and duration for winter mining operations.
Operational interfaces
Dashboards, alerts, and interpretable outputs for operations.
Performance assessment
Validation using field observations and operational outcomes, with uncertainty quantification.

05 / Boundaries

Prediction is not certainty.

Calibration, quality control, field testing, and reproducibility protocols are development priorities.

Predictive functions are validated against independent observations and historical cases. Each application communicates uncertainty, operating limits, and the role of human judgment in safety-critical settings.
