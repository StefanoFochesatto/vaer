Systems Architecture

Initial Product-Family Architecture

Modular product lines engineered to integrate field sensing, low-power mountain communications, and high-performance physics-based predictive algorithms.

Product Family 01 • Hardware & Decision Support

VAER Cryosphere™

TRL: Field Prototype Validated

Designed for avalanche forecasters, transportation corridors, and alpine mining operations. Integrates autonomous sub-surface snow thermal profiling arrays with mountain mesh communications and wind-loading modeling.

Thermal Probe Array

2-meter vertical carbon-composite probe with 8 calibrated precision thermistors. Measures vertical temperature gradients (dT/dz) down to the ground interface to detect kinetic facet weak layers.

Mountain Mesh Gateway

Solar-assisted lithium titanate power stage rated to -45°C. Sub-GHz LoRa mesh node with automated burst satellite failover (Iridium SBD) for zero line-of-sight dropouts.

Forecaster Interface

Interactive web console displaying real-time snowpack stratigraphy, settlement creep rates, simulated wind slab deposition, and probabilistic instability indices.

OPERATING TEMP: -50°C to +40°C
INGESTION LATENCY: < 30 sec
OUTPUT FORMATS: GeoJSON, NetCDF, Webhook
Product Family 02 • Industrial Atmospheric Prognostics

VAER Atmosphere™

TRL: Operational Pilot

Purpose-built for open-pit mining superintendents and environmental safety managers operating in Alaska, Northern Canada, and Scandinavia. Prognoses temperature inversion events and cold-air particulate trapping.

Prognostic Inversion Engine

Ingests local barometric tendencies, radiative cooling fluxes, and synoptic upper-air patterns to compute inversion onset time, cap boundary height, and expected duration in hours.

Pit Microclimate Profiler

Bench-mounted thermodynamic sensor stations along the pit perimeter, tracking thermal decoupling, katabatic drainage streams, and stagnant exhaust pooling.

Mine Dispatch API

Direct REST/MQTT connector for Wenco, Modular, and CAT MineStar dispatch systems to dynamically pace haul fleet shifts before air quality breaches mandatory stop thresholds.

FORECAST HORIZON: 12h to 72h Ahead
UNCERTAINTY ENVELOPE: Calibrated Gaussian CI (90%)
DISPATCH PROTOCOLS: REST, OPC-UA, MQTT
Product Family 03 • Airborne Sounding Systems

VAER AeroSense™

TRL: Flight Tested

Turnkey airborne payload designed for multi-rotor UAV atmospheric soundings. Measures vertical profiles of pressure, temperature, relative humidity, and aerosol size spectra from 0 to 1,500m AGL.

Isokinetic Aerodynamic Inlet

CFD-optimized sampling boom mounted above and ahead of the rotor disc. Eliminates propeller vortex shearing and particle fractionation during vertical climb.

Aerosol Spectrometer (OPC)

Multi-channel optical particle counter recording PM1, PM2.5, and PM10 mass concentrations alongside 16 discrete size distribution channels (0.35µm to 12.4µm).

Fast-Response Thermistors

Miniature glass-bead thermistors with a thermal time constant of less than 80ms, enabling fine-scale boundary-layer inversion inflection detection during rapid 3m/s climbs.

PAYLOAD MASS: 480 g (All Sensors + Battery)
SAMPLING RATE: 10 Hz Georeferenced
COMPATIBILITY: DJI Matrice 300/350, Freefly, Custom UAVs
Product Family 04 • Numerical Physics & Assimilation

VAER Compute™

TRL: Production Engine

The computational engine driving the entire VAER ecosystem. Runs high-resolution terrain-conforming Navier-Stokes wind solvers, variational data assimilation (4D-Var), and probabilistic risk algorithms.

Terrain Wind CFD

Solves steady and unsteady incompressible wind fields over steep DEM topography at down to 10-meter spatial resolution, simulating boundary layer separation and lee-side eddies.

Variational Assimilation (4D-Var)

Blends sparse ground sensor observations and drone soundings into continuous 3D thermodynamic state vectors using physics-constrained gradient optimization.

Cloud / Edge Deployability

Containerized for high-throughput cloud cluster execution or local ruggedized on-site edge servers operating disconnected at remote mine sites.

ARCHITECTURE: C++20 / CUDA / Python Scientific Stack
DEPLOYMENT: Docker / Kubernetes / Local Edge Appliance
API INTERFACE: gRPC, GraphQL, REST, Protobuf
